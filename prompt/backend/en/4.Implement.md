## Key business rules (products)

- Duplicate listing title (`title`) system-wide → **409**
- Price: must be > 0 and ≤ 99999999 → **400**
- `model_id` must belong under the submitted `brand_id`
- **Blocklist** (config in code, not a table): if `title` or `description` contains forbidden words → **400**
  Use this list (token/word check; case-insensitive for English): **`gun`**, **`ปืน`**, **`เฮโรอีน`**
- Successful GET `/v1/api/products/:id` → `view_count` +1
- List: query filters AND together; pagination `page` (0-based), `size`; sort `newest` | `price_asc` | `price_desc` | `most_viewed`

## Product images

- Maximum **4** images per product
- First image is cover when none exists; PATCH cover; after DELETE cover, pick lowest `sort_order` as cover; keep `sort_order` contiguous 0..n
- Deleting a product removes images in DB and files on disk
